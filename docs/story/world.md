# Story and World Notes

Living document. Items marked **(proposal)** are suggestions from the build partner, not yet confirmed by the creator.

## The world: the Reflection at Night (working name)

- **Core rule:** every place has a reflection made from what people felt there. The reflection surfaces at night.
- Setting: a modern Thai city (Bangkok-inspired).
- **(proposal)** Normally the reflection rises only in the deepest hours (about 2 to 4 AM), and most people sleep through it.
- **(proposal)** Places take the shape of their feelings. Strong feelings left behind become spirits, and deep tragedies become bosses. Each boss is the story of something that happened in that place.
- **(proposal)** The reflection is getting stronger and rising earlier, even at sunset. Something is feeding it. This is the central mystery.
- **(proposal)** Game rhythm: by day you explore the real city, meet people and learn a place's history. By night you face that place's reflection.

## Characters

| Name | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Kieth** | Hero | An ordinary high school boy. Not good at studying or sports. Skips class to chase girls and always gets rejected. Has no special power or destiny. |
| **Ju** | Friend | One of Kieth's two loyal friends who always cheer him up. |
| **Tori** | Friend | Kieth's other loyal friend. |
| **Kate** | Classmate | Girl from the next classroom. Kendo athlete who carries a bamboo sword. Had never met Kieth before he asked her out. |

- **Theme (proposal):** you don't need to be special to matter to someone. Kieth grows through his friends and his own courage, not through a hidden power.
- **(proposal)** In combat Kieth starts as the weakest fighter, and his skills come from moments with the others.
- **(proposal)** The reflection pulls in anyone who is present, not chosen people. The four are caught because they stayed late.

## Opening scene

1. Kieth stays late after school, hoping to ask Kate on a date. Ju and Tori wait in another room.
2. Kieth finds Kate alone in her classroom and asks her out. She has never seen him before and rejects him.
3. The sun sets, the school chime rings, and everything goes dark.
4. Something moves. It is Ju and Tori running in, worried about Kieth.
5. Something moves again at the window. It shatters and a monster attacks.
6. Kate draws her bamboo sword from her bag. The other three fight bare-handed.

- This is the first battle and doubles as the combat tutorial, with exactly four fighters.
- **(proposal)** The school's reflection is made of teenage feelings (rejection, loneliness, pressure to be noticed). The first monster echoes the rejection Kieth just felt.
